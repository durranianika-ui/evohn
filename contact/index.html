What we help with

Most asked, most useful

Whatever you write about, you are writing to the team rather than to a queue. No bots, no automated sales sequence.

  • Certificate retrieval and verification

    Every published certificate carries an accession number that the issuing laboratory can retrieve independently. If you need a historical batch, or confirmation direct from the laboratory, the desk will arrange it.

  • Specification and method questions

    Questions about gradient conditions, detection wavelength, or how a particular determination was made are answered with reference to the actual certificate rather than a general statement.

  • Handling and stability

    Compound-specific guidance on reconstitution, diluent choice, aliquoting and reconstituted stability intervals — including where the guidance differs from the generic advice.

  • Custody and logistics

    Cold-chain arrangements, in-transit temperature control, and what to do if an indicator arrives out of range. The answer to the last one is: tell us before you open it.
