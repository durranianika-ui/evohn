The documentation is the reason we moved supplier

We had been buying from a source that would send a certificate on request, eventually, and often for a batch adjacent to the one we had. Being able to pull the exact accession number and confirm it with the laboratory directly removed an entire class of argument from our internal review. The material itself has been consistent across three orders; the paperwork is what changed our procurement decision.

Principal investigator, university laboratory
