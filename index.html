Our Philosophy

Precision before promise. 

A compound earns the EVOHN name only after it has been measured. Purity is determined by analysis and published per lot, identity is confirmed by mass spectrometry, and both are repeated by an accredited laboratory that has no stake in the result.

We exist to remove uncertainty from research. That is a documentation problem before it is a chemistry problem, and we treat it as one.

The EVOHN Standard→
01 / 04

Analytical Purity

Every batch is analysed by HPLC and the measured result is published per lot. A number that is measured, not claimed.

02 / 04

Cold-Chain Integrity

Temperature-controlled from synthesis through to delivery, so that what was verified is what arrives.

03 / 04

Independent Verification

Accredited third-party laboratories confirm in-house analysis. Trust is a consequence of transparency.

04 / 04

Manufacturing Standards

Produced under good manufacturing practice protocols within certified facilities, documented at every stage.

Purity figures quoted anywhere on this site are measured values published per batch on the certificate of analysis — not specifications, and not targets.
